Enemy Collision Synchronized - v2.13.0



Keeps large enemy corpse collisions aligned with their bodies and curbs the

renewed ragdoll movement that can occur in vanilla.



1. Close Helldivers 2. Use Arsenal or HD2MM.

2. Replace the previous Corpse Collision Repair / Enemy Collision Synchronized

   entry with Enemy-Collision-Synchronized-v2.13.0.zip.

3. Import and enable Bingus Shared Loader (v18 or newer) and this mod.

4. With Arsenal default priority, put the loader LAST (bottom of the list).

   If first-mod priority is enabled, put it FIRST instead.

5. Purge, then Deploy. Launch the game normally.



Vanilla Plus Megapack also includes this mod. A current standalone copy can

remain installed alongside the pack; duplicate callbacks do not stack. Give

the pack winning priority to use its bundled version, with one shared loader.



Corpse bodies retain their normal physical collision. The mod aligns displaced

collision pieces, curbs renewed movement after remote corpses settle, and

removes lingering collision from settled Impaler tentacle claws.

Compatible large enemies include Terminids, Automatons and Illuminate.



Performance profiling is off by default. Diagnostic setups can set

CowboyBingusDiagnostics = true before initialization. Then, after a busy

client mission, save

%LOCALAPPDATA%/CowboyBingus/Helldivers2/Logs/EnemyCollisionSynchronized-Performance.log and

%LOCALAPPDATA%/CowboyBingus/Helldivers2/Logs/CorpseCollisionRepair.log before launching another session.

The first log reports recent and cumulative mod costs, the slowest polls,

and costs by enemy/lifecycle, refreshed every ten seconds. Check it starts

with v2.13.0 and schema=2. Work per update remains limited; heavily crowded

scenes can delay corpse checks. Repair behavior is unchanged from v2.7

apart from the re-pose cooldown added in v2.11.0.
